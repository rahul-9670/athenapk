AthenaPK uses Heaviside–Lorentz magnetic units: vA = B/√ρ, Pmag = B²/2 — no 4π anywhere. Converting a code B to Gauss therefore carries the √(4π): BG = Bcode·v₀·√(4πρ₀). This is the #1 analysis pitfall of the project.

One timestep, anatomised

Every cycle of athenaPK executes this task graph on all mesh blocks (Kokkos/CUDA kernels, one MPI rank per GPU):

  1. Ideal MHD — VL2 predictor–corrector, PLM reconstruction, HLLD Riemann solver, GLM–Dedner (dedner_extended) divergence cleaning. CFL 0.3.
  2. Non-ideal MHD — Ohmic + ambipolar fluxes super-time-stepped with RKL2 (ratio cap 1000, η frozen per half-step); the dispersive Hall EMF runs unsplit at its whistler dt with an Ohmic floor 0.05.
  3. M1 radiation — explicit transport sub-cycles at reduced light speed ĉ = c/1000, then implicit local matter–radiation coupling.
  4. Chemistry — gow17-reduced network, semi-implicit, ≤400 sub-steps; returns xe to the non-ideal terms.
  5. Self-gravity — BiCGSTAB, preconditioned by FAS multigrid with SRJ smoothers, tol 10⁻⁶.
  6. AMR — Jeans tagging (refine when λJ < 8Δx), 32³ blocks, derefinement damped over 50 tags.

The physics

A Bonnor–Ebert sphere is the maximum-mass hydrostatic configuration of isothermal, self-gravitating gas confined by external pressure. Its structure solves the isothermal Lane–Emden equation; solutions are stable only out to dimensionless radius ξmax = 6.45. We build the sphere exactly at that critical radius and then multiply the density everywhere by f = 5 — the cloud holds five times the mass its pressure can carry, so collapse is not seeded, it is guaranteed.

(1/ξ²) d/dξ (ξ² dψ/dξ) = e−ψ,   ξ = r·√(4πGρ_c)/c_s Isothermal Lane–Emden equation: the density profile is ρ = ρ_c·e^(−ψ). Critical (marginally stable) boundary at ξ = 6.45.
M_BE ≈ 1.18 · c_s⁴ / (G3/2 P_ext1/2) The Bonnor–Ebert mass: the largest cloud a given sound speed and surface pressure can hold up. Our 6 M☉ at f = 5 is far beyond it.
μ_Φ ≡ (M/Φ) / (M/Φ)_crit,  (M/Φ)_crit = 1/(2π√G)  →  μ_Φ = 31.9 Mass-to-magnetic-flux ratio in units of the critical value. 31.9 ≫ 1: the uniform 7.47 μG field cannot prevent collapse — it is a passenger that gets amplified, twisted, and partially left behind (the "fossil field" question this campaign studies).
t_ff = √(3π / (32 G ρ_c)) = 40.3 kyr Free-fall time at the initial centre (2.73×10⁻¹⁸ g/cm³) — the clock every later stage is measured against.

On top of the sphere: solid-body rotation with Ω·tff = 0.02 (slow, realistic), and a turbulent velocity field at Mach 0.5 with spectrum P(k) ∝ k−11/3 over modes k = 1–8 — enough to break symmetry, not enough to support the cloud.

How AthenaPK builds it
  • src/pgen/collapse_be.cpp integrates the Lane–Emden ODE at startup (BEProfile), places the sphere with rc_code = 6.45, ambient density = f·BE(rc) outside, and zeroes momentum outside the sphere every step (fixed velocity boundary).
  • Input block <problem/collapse_be>: mass=6, temperature=10, f=5, B0z=0.15, omegatff=0.02, turb_mach=0.5, turb_alpha=3.667, turb_seed=42.
  • Turbulent kicks are mean-subtracted — a 2026-07 audit found the un-subtracted field kicked the whole cloud at 0.34 cs.
  • Chemistry scalars are seeded atomic (xH=1); H₂ then forms on grains en route (reaction R1 below).
  • Domain: (52 l₀)³ = (0.47 pc)³, 256³ root grid, outflow BCs — the box is 4× wider than the sphere so the zero-Dirichlet gravity boundary cannot clip the potential (the L=16 matched box famously froze; multipole BCs now solve that, but this run predates them and uses the big box).
Measured at t = 0 — snapshot 00000: ρ_max = 4.97 code = 2.72×10⁻¹⁸ g/cm³ (the profile-discretised f·ρ₀), 512 blocks, single level. Mass on grid 5.0913×10⁴ code, conserved to the last history dump.

The physics

While the gas is optically thin, every erg of compression heat is radiated away by dust within years — collapse at fixed 10 K. That makes the Jeans mass shrink as density grows, so collapse begets faster collapse: a genuine runaway. The centre races ahead of the envelope, building the universal ρ ∝ r⁻² isothermal envelope; density grows slowly for tens of kyr and then explosively in the last centuries.

λ_J = c_s √(π/(Gρ)) ,   M_J ∝ T3/2 ρ−1/2 Jeans length/mass. At fixed T, M_J falls as ρ^(−1/2): each factor 100 in density makes 10× less mass unstable — the definition of runaway.
Truelove: Δx ≤ λ_J / 8  (everywhere, always) Resolving the Jeans length by ≥4 cells prevents artificial fragmentation; we hold 8. This single criterion drives the entire AMR hierarchy.
B ∝ ρκ, κ ≈ 1/2–2/3;   η_A = B² / (4π γ_AD ρ_i ρ) Flux-freezing amplifies B with density; ambipolar diffusion (neutrals slipping past the ion-electron fluid, drag coefficient γ_AD) lets the inner cloud shed magnetic flux. η_A grows as B²/ρ_i·ρ — strongest exactly where ionization is lowest.

The magnetic field also carries angular momentum outward (magnetic braking) — with μΦ = 31.9 it shapes the collapse geometry (pseudo-disk, field pinching into an hourglass) without ever halting it.

How AthenaPK computes it
  • All physics is on from t = 0 — there is no staged switch-on. During this phase the timestep is set by the ordinary hydro CFL.
  • Jeans refinement (refinement/type=jeans, njeans=8) opened levels 1→10 during this stage (512 → 1247 blocks). With 4πG = 1 and cs = 1 the criterion is simply λ_J = 2π/√ρ < 8Δx.
  • Self-gravity solves ∇²φ = ρ/1 (i.e. 4πGρ) every cycle: BiCGSTAB wrapped around an FAS multigrid preconditioner (SRJ smoothers), residual 10⁻⁶.
  • The ionization module (ionization.hpp) tracks xe(ρ,T) from cosmic-ray ionization (ζ) balanced by recombination and grain capture across MRN grain-size bins; chemistry supplies xe live via passive scalar 4 (ambipolar_coeff=ionization_chem, xe_scalar_index=4).
Measured — the peak crossed ρ_crit = 10⁻¹³ g/cm³ between t = 50.35 and 50.58 kyr (snapshots 10–25), i.e. at ≈ 1.25 free-fall times, with AMR already at level 7; the final decades of the isothermal climb take only centuries. The ρ ∝ r⁻² envelope it leaves behind is visible in the final radial profile (Figure 3).

The physics

Near 10⁻¹³ g cm⁻³ the dusty gas becomes optically thick to its own cooling radiation: compression heat is trapped, pressure finally rises fast enough to fight gravity, and the collapse stalls into a quasi-hydrostatic object a few AU across — the first core. It sits behind an accretion shock, swallowing the infalling envelope, contracting slowly along an adiabat while its temperature climbs three orders of magnitude.

τ = κ ρ R ≫ 1  →  T ∝ ργ−1 Optically-thick ⇒ adiabatic. γ = 5/3 while H₂ rotation is frozen (T < θ_rot = 85.4 K), then 7/5 once rotational levels carry energy. The EOS table encodes the transition exactly.
κ_dust = 2×10⁻⁴ T² cm²/g (T < 167 K) … Bell & Lin power laws … sublimation at 1500 K Gray opacity: ice-mantled grains at low T, successive Bell & Lin (1994) regimes above, dust gone at 1500 K.
∂E_r/∂t + ∇·F_r = ĉ ρκ (a_r T⁴ − E_r)  (+ flux eq. with M1 closure) M1 moment radiation transport: evolves radiation energy and flux with a closure that is exact in both free-streaming and diffusion limits — the first core's radiative structure is emergent, not prescribed.
η_O = c²/(4πσ_O), η_H = c²σ_H/(4πσ_⊥²), η_A = B²/(4πγ_AD ρ_i ρ) The three non-ideal diffusivities from the Wardle conductivity tensor (σ_O, σ_H, σ_P) built from electrons, ions, and charged MRN grains. In the first core x_e collapses and the field decouples: ambipolar dominates the envelope, Ohmic the interior, and the SIGNED Hall term sets the spin-up/spin-down asymmetry of the future disk.

This is where non-ideal MHD earns its keep: flux that stayed frozen-in through Stage 1 is deposited here ("magnetic decoupling"), deciding how much field the eventual star inherits — the fossil-field question.

How AthenaPK computes it
  • Tabulated multi-Saha EOS (eos=hydrogen, table from gen_eos_table.py): H₂ ⇌ 2H, H ⇌ H⁺+e⁻, He ⇌ He⁺ ⇌ He⁺⁺ solved jointly at each (ρ,e); includes H₂ rotation/vibration (θ_rot = 85.4 K, θ_vib = 5987 K), X = 0.716. P, c_s², T interpolated bilinearly on-device.
  • M1 radiation (src/radiation/): reduced speed of light ĉ = c/creduc with creduc = 1000 (ĉ_code = 1580); implicit matter coupling keeps the gas–radiation exchange stable. With RT active the pgen's old barotropic overwrite is skipped — thermal evolution is fully emergent (verified in source).
  • Ionization tensor (ionization.hpp): OML grain charging over MRN bins, thermal potassium (Saha) ionization above ~10³ K, grain sublimation; returns signed η_H — the electron-only Hall formula over-predicts catastrophically at the ionization minimum, which this tensor fixes.
  • dt survival kit: RKL2 super-time-stepping for the parabolic terms (ratio cap 1000, η frozen per Strang half-step), Hall unsplit with Ohmic floor 0.05, and the user-approved η_O cap 0.1 in the diffusion-decoupled interior (probe-validated: 6.1× throughput, physics unchanged where diffusion matters).
Measured — the run shows the textbook plateau: between cycles 15,000 and 67,500 the peak density crawled 2.9×10⁻¹¹ → 1.26×10⁻¹⁰ g/cm³ (23 yr of quasi-hydrostatic accretion at level 10) before the next runaway. The raw central temperature readout at the stop is ~587 K, but this is contaminated low by the STS/radiation bug (caveat below); the exact-EOS isentrope from the last clean state gives ~1500–2200 K. The central ionization fraction sits at the 10⁻¹⁵ floor — deeply decoupled — though a hotter corrected core may partially re-couple via thermal (K) ionization: a first-order question for the fossil-flux budget, answered by the re-run.

The physics

At ~2000 K collisions begin tearing H₂ apart. Each dissociation swallows 4.478 eV — enormous against kT ≈ 0.17 eV — so the energy of further compression goes into breaking bonds instead of raising pressure. The effective adiabatic index drops to ≈1.1, below the critical 4/3, and hydrostatic support becomes impossible: the first core's interior goes back into free-fall. Six more decades of density in a few years.

H₂ ⇌ 2H:  K_d = (π m_H kT/h²)3/2·16·(Z_rot Z_vib)⁻¹·e−χ_d/kT,  χ_d = 4.478 eV The dissociation Saha equation the EOS table solves (coupled with H and He ionization). The latent-heat sink is what pins T near 2000 K while ρ races.
stability requires γ_eff > 4/3;  dissociating H₂ ⇒ γ_eff ≈ 1.1 < 4/3 ⇒ collapse Virial/Chandrasekhar criterion for self-gravitating gas: below 4/3, added compression cannot raise pressure fast enough to matter.
dt_whistler ∝ Δx² / |η_H| The computational villain of this stage: Hall physics is dispersive (whistler waves, ω ∝ k²), so its explicit timestep shrinks with the square of resolution — and |η_H| ~ B/(e n_e) runs away in the ionization minimum. This is what the η_O/η_H caps and STS machinery exist to survive.
How AthenaPK computes it
  • The multi-Saha EOS is the second collapse: γ_eff(ρ,T) dips below 4/3 across the dissociation band automatically; the code has no switch to flip.
  • Radiation enters the diffusion limit; M1's closure handles the transition smoothly and the implicit coupling absorbs the stiff emission term.
  • AMR follows the runaway: levels 13 and 14 opened within 3 h of each other (blocks 2486 → 2843); at level 14 the finest cell is 0.023 AU = 5.0 R☉.
  • The step is whistler-limited (dt ≈ 5–7×10⁻⁹ code ≈ 2–3 h of physical time per step). The η_H magnitude cap (binary v5, structurally identical to the η_O cap: signed clamp, ionization family only) was implemented to bound it; two pricing probes are queued.
Measured — runaway resumption logged 2026-07-13 08:43 at 1.65×10⁻⁸ g/cm³; density rose 61× in the following 26.5 h of wall time; run front at stop: ρ_max = 1.44×10⁻⁷ g/cm³ (1.44×10⁶ × ρ_crit), cycle 77,250, t = 1.08399 code. Density e-folding time measured across the final snapshots: 0.35 yr — a genuine dynamical runaway. Infall reaches 2.5 km/s (13 cs) at r = 0.35 AU. Preliminary: this entire runaway segment ran with the STS/radiation bug (see caveat in the results section) whose artificial cooling kept the readout at ~590 K; the exact-EOS isentrope puts the true central temperature near 1500–2200 K — at or approaching H₂-dissociation onset. The segment is being re-simulated with the fixed binary from the last clean checkpoint (cycle 71,000); onset density, e-fold time, and end-state values here will be finalized then.

The physics (Larson 1969; Masunaga & Inutsuka 2000)

Once H₂ is fully dissociated the energy sink closes; γ recovers toward 5/3 (with a second, shallower dip at hydrogen ionization near 10⁴ K), pressure wins again, and a second hydrostatic object forms at roughly stellar density and stellar radius: the second core — by definition, the protostar. A second accretion shock processes everything that falls on it from here on. Deuterium ignites later at T_c ≈ 10⁶ K.

γ_eff: 1.1 → 5/3 (H₂ gone) → dip < 4/3 (H ionization, ~10⁴ K) → 5/3 (plasma) The EOS table's γ_eff(T) validation curve shows exactly this sequence — the physics that ends the collapse is already in the table (checked at generation time).
How AthenaPK will handle it
  • Resolution is provisioned: numlevel=20 gives a finest cell of 0.16 R☉, and the input deck's own comment records that Truelove (njeans = 8) holds through second-core formation at ~10⁻² g/cm³.
  • Past that, the interior becomes unresolvable and dt collapses for good — the exit is the sink particle package (src/sinks/, Parthenon swarm): creation at a verified density peak, symplectic N-body, accretion with machine-precision mass/momentum bookkeeping, restart- and AMR-safe, GPU smoke-tested.
  • Honest caveat from validation (WS-1, gate 4): in a self-gravitating collapse a sink holds dt 2.7–3.6× above the no-sink control but does not fully restore it — infall replenishes the accretion zone faster than it clears. Sinks are a bounded lever, not a magic exit.
Status — ~11 density e-folds beyond the current front (ln(10⁻²/1.44×10⁻⁷) ≈ 11.1). At the pre-stop pace (~12–14 h of wall per e-fold) that is on the order of a GPU-week away — the reason the Hall-cap retooling now under way is worth its pause.

The physics

The protostar now grows by eating its envelope while radiating away gravitational energy. Disk, outflows and radiative feedback regulate the diet. When the envelope is gone, the star contracts on the Kelvin–Helmholtz timescale until the core reaches hydrogen-fusion temperature — the zero-age main sequence. For 6 M☉, t_KH is comparable to the accretion time, so an intermediate-mass star arrives at the ZAMS almost as soon as it stops accreting (it may even ignite while still accreting).

Ṁ ~ c_s³/G ≈ 1.6×10⁻⁶ M☉/yr (at 10 K; boosted by the f = 5 envelope) Shu (1977) self-similar accretion rate — the envelope's mass supply, set only by the sound speed of the parent cloud.
t_KH = G M² / (R L) ≈ 4×10⁵ yr  (M = 6 M☉, R ≈ 3 R☉, L ≈ 10³ L☉) Kelvin–Helmholtz: how long gravity can pay the radiative bill. Short for 6 M☉ — massive stars reach the main sequence fast.
How this maps onto AthenaPK
  • This stage is carried by sink + M1 feedback, not by resolving the star: the sink accretes and its environment is heated through the radiation field. A production "star-in-the-box" run to ~10⁵ yr is the sink package's design target.
  • Interior stellar evolution (Hayashi/Henyey tracks, ignition) is the domain of 1-D stellar evolution codes; the MHD run's deliverable is the star's birth state: mass, spin, and — the campaign's science goal — its fossil magnetic flux.

Known issue (found 2026-07-15; fixed in binary v6; impact revised 2026-07-16): the RKL2 super-time-stepping kernels packed all Independent variables — including rad.Er/Fr and grav.phi — and overwrote them each STS call with a snapshot container that only receives cons/prim. From cycle 71,000 (when integrator=rkl2 entered production) every cycle destroyed the radiation field; the matter coupling rebuilt it, and in the reduced-speed-of-light bookkeeping that rebuild charges the gas c/ĉ = 1000× the radiation energy — an artificial cooling channel worth 30–70% of the compression heating during the runaway. Proven three ways: the exact-EOS isentrope from the last clean state reaches ~2200 K at the final density versus the contaminated ~590 K readout; the restart transient matches the predicted 1000·aT⁴ charge; the pre-bug Er sits exactly at aT⁴. Consequence: all thermodynamics between cycles 71,000–77,250 (the L11–L14 runaway) are contaminated low; the true core temperature at the front is near-adiabatic (~1500–2200 K, dissociation imminent or under way). Everything through cycle 71,000 — including the plateau — is clean. The fix (pack STS kernels by name, {"cons"}) is validated; the runaway segment is being re-simulated with binary v6 from the last clean checkpoint (cycle 71,000, healthy Er).
